# Code of conduct

Virga follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([Tresillo2017](https://github.com/Tresillo2017)), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn or ban participants who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
